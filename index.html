About

I'm a PhD student in Natural Language Processing at the Univesity of Edinburgh (Sept 2025 - present) working under the supervision of Björn Ross, Emily Allaway, and Ivan Titov. I research how language models encode and represent generalizations in language (e.g., "PhD students are curious"), with applications areas including detecting stereotypes (generalizations about groups of people) and over-exaggerated claims in scientific communication.

Before my PhD, I worked as a software engineer with Amazon's Alexa Speech team in Cambridge, UK (2022-2025). I have a Masters in Speech and Language Processing from the University of Edinburgh (2021-2022) and a Bachelors in Mathematics and Computer Science (minor in Linguistics) from the University of Maryland, College Park (2017-2021).
